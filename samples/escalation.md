# Incident escalation and service levels

All content is synthetic demonstration policy.

A priority-one (P1) support incident means a production service is unavailable. The support team must acknowledge a P1 incident within 15 minutes, page the on-call incident commander immediately, and open an incident bridge. Notify the customer's designated contact and provide updates every 30 minutes until service is restored. If there is no mitigation after 60 minutes, escalate to the engineering director. Preserve an incident timeline and complete a post-incident review within five business days. Never promise a restoration time without an approved engineering estimate.

For P2 incidents, acknowledge within four business hours and assign the owning support engineer. For P3 questions, respond within one business day. These are response targets, not guarantees of resolution. Business hours are Monday through Friday, 09:00–17:00 Eastern Time, excluding published holidays.
