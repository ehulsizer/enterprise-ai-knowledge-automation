# Account and service guide

All content is synthetic demonstration policy.

Customer administrators manage account membership and role assignments. Require identity verification through the approved account recovery process before resetting access. Never request passwords or API secrets in a support ticket. An enterprise customer can designate two escalation contacts. Order status values are processing, in transit, delivered, and cancelled. Expected delivery dates in the operational API are estimates. Policy documents cannot establish the current status of an order; read the operational service for that information.
