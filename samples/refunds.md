# Subscription refund policy

All content is synthetic demonstration policy.

Enterprise subscriptions are non-refundable after the first 30 calendar days of the initial term. Within the initial 30 days, a customer may request a prorated refund for unused service by opening a billing ticket. Renewals are non-refundable unless the signed order form provides otherwise. Billing must verify the subscription start date and contractual exceptions before approving a refund. Support agents cannot authorize refunds directly. Service credits for SLA breaches require a separate review and are not cash refunds. Do not infer eligibility from customer tier alone.
